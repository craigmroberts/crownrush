What costs what

Tickets carry a model and effort line, so the price of doing one is on the ticket rather than guessed. Rough shape: Haiku for mechanical passes, Sonnet 5 for ordinary build-and-verify, Opus 5 for anything where being wrong is expensive, Fable 5.1 only for decisions with long tails — the story, an opening, a name you will live with. Judge cost per finished job, not per request.
