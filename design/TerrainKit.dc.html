Terrain kit

The set pieces the map is built from. Each becomes a low-poly 3D piece with the same colours.

River

Sandy banks, two-tone water, drifting foam lines. Blocks all walking.

Bridge

Plank deck with railings. Bridges are the only crossings, so each is a natural choke point for a tower.

Curved road

Spline ribbon with a darker shoulder and wheel ruts. Replaces the straight strips.

Mesas and peaks

Stepped grey mesas with grass tops, snow-capped peaks behind for depth.

Pines, bushes, hay

Three-tier pines in two sizes, round bushes, hay bales near the fields.

Wheat field

Golden rows with a low fence. Sways gently; purely for life and colour.

Boulders, barricades, flowers

Faceted rocks with a lit top face, crossed-stake barricades, grass tufts and flowers.

Ground texture

Soft lighter and darker patches and grass tufts instead of one flat green, matching the mottled meadow in your reference shots.
