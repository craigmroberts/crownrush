Raiders, redesigned

Same chibi build as your archers, but every raider gets its own silhouette so you can read a wave at a glance.

Archer (yours)

Reference for scale and build. Rounded head half the height, outlined shapes, flat cel colour.

Raider

Round red kettle helmet with nose guard and plume, small round shield, short sword. The common foot soldier.
20 HP · 6 dmg · fast

Elite

Black plate with steel pauldrons, full-face great helm with a glowing visor slit, red crest, longsword. Arrives from wave 8.
60 HP · 12 dmg

Brute

Barrel body, bare arms, horned cap and a studded club. Slow, wide and mean; drops more coin.
70 HP · 14 dmg · slow

Giant (boss)

Bone-white colossus with a pink underbelly, a horned helm, leather chest strap and a two-handed greatsword. Sweeps everything nearby and hammers walls.
420 HP · 20 dmg · every 5th wave
Shape language. Raider = round, Elite = tall and angular, Brute = wide, Giant = huge. Read the wave from silhouettes alone.
Colour. Enemies keep to red, black and pinkish bone so they never read as your blue-and-white army. Eyes turn to glowing slits on the armoured ones.
Animation. Walk cycles already exist; adding a wind-up lean before each strike and a stagger on hit makes them feel heavier.
