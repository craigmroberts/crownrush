World map, proposed

Top-down plan of the whole play area. Village at centre, raiders arrive along the roads.
Rivers wind across the map as natural walls. Raiders can only cross at the two wooden bridges, which become choke points to guard; the King crosses the same way.
Roads are curved splines with a darker shoulder and wheel ruts, meeting at the village gates. Raiders follow them in, so you can read an attack from the road it uses.
Mesas stack in the north-west with snow-capped peaks behind them. Forests, boulders, barricades, a wheat field and flower clusters fill the meadow between.
